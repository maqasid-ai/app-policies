# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

Maqasid AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application, Ruju (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 App Usage Data
The App collects minimal information necessary to provide and improve functionality:

- **Reading Position**: Your current Mushaf page (1-604) and Ayah Reader position, so you can resume where you left off
- **Reading Goals and Streaks**: Your daily goal (in ayahs, pages, or rukus), streak history, grace days, and khatm (full-Quran completion) cycle progress
- **Points and Badges**: Rewards earned through reading, used only to display your own progress back to you
- **Bookmarks and Notes**: Any bookmarks, bookmark folders, and personal notes you create within the App
- **Search History**: Text you've searched for within the App, to show your recent searches
- **Display Preferences**: Your chosen translation (English or Urdu), tajweed color display, split-view setting, and theme (light/dark/system)
- **Reminder Settings**: Whether you've enabled the streak reminder, night reminder, or weekly recap, and their scheduled times

### 1.2 Device Information
The App may access the following device information:

- **Device Calendar Settings**: To determine your device's time format (12-hour or 24-hour) when you set a reminder time

### 1.3 Device Permissions
The App requests the following device permissions, used only for the purposes below:

- **Notifications**: To show your local reminders (streak reminder, night reminder, weekly recap)
- **Device Restart**: To ensure your local reminders keep working after your device restarts
- **Vibrate**: For notification vibration

The App does **not** request Internet or network access on any platform. It cannot make a network request under any circumstance.

### 1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect
We do **not** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
- Account information (the App does not require user accounts)
- Location data
- Biometric data
- Payment information
- Contact lists or other personal data from your device
- Browsing history or activity outside the App

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

- **App Functionality**: To remember your reading position, goals, streaks, bookmarks, and preferences so you can continue where you left off
- **Rewards and Reminders**: To track your own reading progress and badges, and to schedule the local reminders you've enabled
- **User Experience**: To provide a personalized experience by maintaining your preferences across app sessions

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage
All information collected by the App is stored **locally on your device**. This means:

- Your data never leaves your device
- We do not transmit your information to external servers
- Your reading positions, goals, bookmarks, and preferences are stored only on your device

### 3.2 Data Security
We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

- Data is stored using secure local storage provided by the operating system
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- The App operates entirely offline after initial installation

### 3.3 Data Retention
Your preferences and reading positions are retained on your device until:

- You uninstall the App (which will delete all stored data)
- You manually clear the App's data through your device settings
- You reset your device

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does **not** use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do **not** share, sell, or rent your information to any third parties. As noted in Section 1.3, the App does not request Internet or network access on any platform, so it is incapable of making a network request under any circumstance.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect personal information and operates entirely offline, it is safe for users of all ages.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

- **Access**: You can view your reading progress, goals, bookmarks, and preferences within the App itself
- **Modification**: You can change your translation, display, and reminder preferences at any time through the App's settings
- **Deletion**: You can delete individual bookmarks and notes within the App, or delete all App data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings
- **Control**: You control what reminders are scheduled, and what you bookmark, note, or search for

## 7. Offline Functionality

The App is designed to work **entirely offline** after installation. All Quran text, translations, mushaf page layout data, and fonts are bundled with the App and do not require an internet connection. This means:

- No data is transmitted while using the App
- Your reading activity is completely private
- The App functions without any network connectivity

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted.
